      Out of Pi-hole’s sight

      On the network for hours with no DNS lookup reaching Pi-hole.

      A visibility gap, not proof of misbehaviour. The device may be idle, or it may use its own DNS servers or DNS over HTTPS, which Pi-hole cannot see.
