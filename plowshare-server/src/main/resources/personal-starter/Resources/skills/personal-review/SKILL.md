---
name: personal-review
description: Review requested Personal goals, responsibilities or a time period, surface progress and stalled decisions, and propose practical next steps. Use for daily, weekly or focused reviews on request.
mode: DIRECT
---
Resolve authorized Personal access and establish the requested period or focus.
Read relevant plans, knowledge changes and selected captures. Ground progress in
actual records; do not treat a planned action as completed or absence of a record
as proof that nothing happened.

Return a short view of progress, open decisions, stalled work, relevant new material
and a manageable next step. Use skill_read for references/review.md if helpful.
Update plan state when the request covers it. Archive completed or inactive material
only when requested; preserve retrieval links and references when moving it.

Save a requested review in Out and record meaningful durable changes in the log.
Unprocessed captures may be highlighted, but this review does not silently ingest
the entire Inbox. A weekly review request runs once; recurring reviews require the
user's scheduling instruction through existing Plowshare infrastructure.
